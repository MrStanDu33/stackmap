# Accessibility

Stackmap targets **RGAA 4.1 / WCAG 2.1 AA**, with a strict mode for users who need more.

## Checked automatically

- Every story runs as a test in Chromium, in each theme (midnight, light), contrast mode (standard, strict) and locale
  (en, fr). A story fails when its play function fails or when axe reports a violation.
- Token contrast is checked by unit tests and, on the live CSS, by **Foundations/Color tokens**.
- ESLint runs `eslint-plugin-vuejs-accessibility` on every component.

Automated checks find roughly a third of the issues. Keyboard and screen-reader checks are still needed.

## Rules for components

- Every interactive element is reachable and usable with the keyboard, with a visible focus.
- Every control has an accessible name: a visible label, or `aria-label` when the label cannot be visible.
- Do not convey information by color alone: pair status colors with an icon or text.
- Every string goes through i18n, in English and French.
- Every visual graph or canvas has an accessible equivalent (table or form).

## Strict mode

Enabled per user with `data-contrast="strict"` on `<html>`:

- text contrast of at least 7:1, UI elements at least 4.5:1;
- no animation or transition;
- focus always visible, including after a mouse click;
- targets of at least 44 × 44 px.

## Motion

Animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: they are reduced to an instant change. Never rely on an animation to
convey information.
